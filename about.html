Kratie Province (ខេត្តក្រចេះ)

Kratie Province is one of Cambodia's northeastern provinces, famous for its beautiful natural scenery, the Mekong River, freshwater dolphins, historical sites, and traditional Cambodian culture. It is a peaceful province where people enjoy a quiet lifestyle surrounded by nature.

  • General Information
    • Location: Northeastern Cambodia
    • Provincial Capital: Kratie City
    • Area: Approximately 11,094 km²
    • Main River: Mekong River
    • Main Occupations: Agriculture, fishing, and tourism
  • Natural Attractions
    • Kampi Dolphin Pool
    • Koh Trong Island
    • Kampi Rapids
    • Phnom Sambok
  • Culture and Traditions
    • Traditional Khmer houses
    • Buddhist traditions and festivals
    • Traditional fishing activities
    • Indigenous communities
  • Economy and Agriculture
    • Rice farming
    • Cashew nuts
    • Rubber production
    • Fruit farming
    • Fishing
  • Local Food and Specialties
    • Koh Trong pomelo
    • Bamboo-cooked sticky rice (Kralan)
    • Freshwater fish dishes
    • Traditional Khmer food
